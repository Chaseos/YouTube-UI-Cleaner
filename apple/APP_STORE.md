## Name

UI Cleaner for YouTube

## Subtitle

Cleaner YouTube browsing

## Description

Customize what you see on YouTube.

UI Cleaner for YouTube is an independent browser extension for Mac that lets you hide unwanted interface elements and choose a cleaner browsing experience.

Hide Shorts, Mixes, podcasts, playlists, live streams, and promotional elements. Create keyword filters to hide unwanted topics, and choose which cleanup options to enable.

Enable the extension in your browser’s extension settings, allow access to youtube.com, and use the toolbar popup to adjust your preferences. Your preferences stay on this Mac.

All cleanup features are available without payment. Optional tips support development and unlock no additional features.

UI Cleaner for YouTube is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
