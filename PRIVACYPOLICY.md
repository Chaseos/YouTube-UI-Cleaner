# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** December 17, 2025

## Data Collection

The **YouTube UI Cleaner** extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It operates entirely locally on your device.

## Permissions

-   **Storage**: Used to save your preference settings locally (e.g., whether you want to hide Shorts or Paid Promotions).
-   **Scripting / ActiveTab**: Used to modify the CSS and HTML on YouTube.com to hide specific elements based on your settings.

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
